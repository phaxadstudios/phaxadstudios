## About Me

I'm **Israel Nnamchi**, a technology entrepreneur, software developer and founder of **[APAXAA TECHNOLOGIES LTD](https://www.apaxaa.com)**.

My work focuses on building technology-driven businesses, developing digital products and exploring solutions across fintech, business technology, financial markets and software infrastructure.

I enjoy working across the entire product journey — from identifying opportunities and designing systems to developing, launching and growing products.
