## 🏢 Ventures & Products

### APAXAA
**Technology & Innovation**

[APAXAA Technologies LTD](https://www.apaxaa.com) is the parent company behind a growing portfolio of technology ventures and digital products. Its focus is on building, developing, and scaling solutions across different industries.

### VERNSA
**Business Technology & Digital Commerce**

[VERNSA](https://vernsa.com) is a Platform which goal is to enable a global means of transaction , one without stress , cheap, reliable, strong, fast and secured.

### JOYET
**Fintech & Digital Payments**

[JOYET](https://joyet.site) is a financial technology product focused on making cryptocurrency more useful in everyday financial activities by connecting digital assets with practical payment and financial services.

### CANDLEBEN
**Financial Markets & Trading Technology**

[Candleben](https://candleben.com) is a trading research and backtesting platform that helps traders replay historical markets, test strategies, and evaluate trading ideas before applying them in live markets.

Its mission is to make trading strategy research and backtesting more accessible.
